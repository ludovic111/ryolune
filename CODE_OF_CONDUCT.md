# Code of conduct

ryolune follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1:
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html>.

In short: be kind and patient, welcome people of every background and level of experience,
give and take feedback about the work and not the person, and leave out harassment, insults
and other people's private information. This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews and
anywhere else you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour makes the project unwelcoming, tell the maintainer privately through
[a private security advisory](https://github.com/ludovic111/ryolune/security/advisories/new)
(it reaches only the maintainer) or by email to the address on the maintainer's GitHub
profile. Reports are read promptly and kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments,
commits, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may block people who keep
doing so, following the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
