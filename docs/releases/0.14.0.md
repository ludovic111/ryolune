# ryolune 0.14.0

ryolune now looks like the rest of lsuite: black and white, square, with film grain behind the
chrome. It also opens songs from other music apps, remembers your agent conversations, lets you
steer the agent while it works, and tells you what changed after an update and what went wrong
when something breaks.

## The lsuite look, version 2

- **Black and white.** Everything in the window is a grey. The accent is the ink of the mode:
  white in the dark, black in the light. What you choose is inverted: the open tab, the selected
  tool, a lit key, the menu item under the pointer. Red only means recording, record arm or an
  error. Your track colours stay on your clips and notes.
- **Square, with grain.** No more rounded corners (knobs stay round). Menus and dialogs cast a
  hard shadow, and dialogs and the agent's message box sit inside viewfinder corners. Behind the
  panels is a page of film grain with two corners of dithered light. The arrangement, editors and
  mixer stay solid, and lanes after the song's end are hatched.
- **Chakra Petch** for the interface and IBM Plex Mono for numbers and times, both bundled.
- **Every area has a title bar** that says what is in it (`12 items`, `track 2 · midi`,
  `Bass verse · bars 5 – 12`), with its actions on the right.
- **Tools are grouped** in boxes by kind, in the title bar, the transport and above the
  arrangement. A tool shows its label when there is room and its icon when the window is narrow;
  the tooltip always gives its name and shortcut.
- **Track headers** have a number chip, the whole name on up to two lines, and mute, solo, arm and
  monitoring keys that are always visible.
- **A new mark and app icon**: the ring and the dot cut square, the ring's shadow side dissolving
  into dither.
- Dark, Light and Auto, checked for contrast on every surface over the grain and over white and
  black desktops.

## Coming from another app

- **A first-run setup** asks which app you made music in, whether you want the AI features, lets
  you connect an agent provider and check the sound output, then starts you on the demo song, an
  empty song or a song from your old app. Help › Set Up ryolune… shows it again. If you used
  ryolune before, it counts as done.
- **DAWproject import and export** (Bitwig Studio, Studio One, Cubase 14 and others): tracks,
  buses and sends, volume, pan, mute and solo, MIDI clips with notes and controllers, audio clips
  with their audio and fades, markers, the tempo and its changes, volume and pan automation, and
  plugins with their settings. CLAP, VST3 and Audio Unit plugins load when they are installed.
- **File › Import from Another App…** opens a DAWproject, a MIDI file or a set of stems as a new
  song. **File › Export for Another App…** writes a DAWproject, or for apps without it a folder
  with the MIDI and one WAV per track. Both end with a report of what came across, what changed
  and what was left out.
- Step-by-step instructions for Ableton Live, Logic Pro, FL Studio, Bitwig Studio, REAPER,
  Cubase, Studio One, Pro Tools and GarageBand are in the user guide and in `session.formats`.
- **File › Open Recent…** lists the songs you opened lately, and says when one was moved or
  deleted.

## The agent

- **Conversations are saved with the song.** Each song keeps its own conversations, and they come
  back when you open it again. Click the title at the top of the panel to switch, start a new one
  (the old one is kept), rename or delete one.
- **Project memory**: notes the agent reads before every request, such as the key, the style and
  what to leave alone (up to 32 KB). Only you can change it.
- **Steer while it works.** Type while the agent runs: Send becomes Steer, with Stop beside it.
  The agent reads your note at its next step and keeps what it has done. Claude Code restarts its
  run with your note; the others take it without stopping.
- **Zenith · lsuite** is a new provider: the agents you use in zenith work on your song through
  ryolune's MCP server, in a zenith thread of their own, and their edits show in Changes.

## Updates, what's new and problems

- **What's New**: the first time a new version starts, a sheet lists what changed in every
  release since the one you had. Help › What's New, the command palette and Settings › Updates
  open it again.
- ryolune checks for updates again **every six hours** while it stays open, and **Restart now**
  starts an installed update (asking to save first).
- **Logs and crash reports**: ryolune keeps a log of its last four runs and writes a report when it
  runs into a bug, or when it did not quit properly last time. **Settings › Diagnostics** lists
  them, shows the log and opens the folders. Nothing leaves your computer.
- **Report a Problem** (Help menu) opens a GitHub issue with your version and system filled in,
  for you to read and submit. **Copy diagnostics** copies what a report needs, without keys,
  prompts or songs.

## For scripts and agents

- New commands: `session.formats`, `session.importFrom`, `session.exportTo` (also
  `project.formats`, `project.importFrom`, `project.exportTo`, the names kimchi uses),
  `app.onboarding`, `app.finishOnboarding`, `app.recent`, `app.openRecent`,
  `agent.conversations`, `agent.newConversation`, `agent.selectConversation`,
  `agent.renameConversation`, `agent.deleteConversation`, `agent.memory`, `agent.setMemory`,
  `agent.steer`, `app.logs`, `app.crashReports`, `app.clearCrashReports`, `app.diagnostics`,
  `app.reportProblem`, `app.whatsNew` and `app.relaunch` (alias `app.restart`).
- Refused to agents: `app.finishOnboarding`, `agent.setMemory`, `agent.deleteConversation` and
  `app.reportProblem`. Importing and exporting follow the agent's file and replace-song
  permissions.
- New docs: `docs/README.md` (an index), `ARCHITECTURE.md`, `CONFIGURATION.md` (every setting and
  environment variable) and `SESSION_FORMAT.md` (the `.ryolune` file). The repository has issue
  templates, `SECURITY.md` and `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`.

## Limits

- DAWproject does not carry clip gain, fade curves, automation of plugin parameters, looped clips
  (written out repeat by repeat), time-stretched audio or meter changes. Other apps choose their
  own sound for ryolune's built-in instruments and effects.
- Ableton Live, Logic Pro, FL Studio, REAPER, Pro Tools and GarageBand exchange songs through MIDI
  and stems, not their own project files.
